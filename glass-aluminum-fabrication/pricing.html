What Affects Price

Pricing Factors

Several factors influence the final cost of your project.

Size

Larger dimensions increase material and fabrication costs.

Glass Thickness

Thicker glass provides more strength at higher cost.

Frame Material

Different aluminum alloys and finishes affect pricing.

Glass Type

Tempered, laminated, and special glass have different rates.

Hardware

Premium locks, handles, and hinges affect overall cost.

Installation

Complex installations may require additional labor and time.

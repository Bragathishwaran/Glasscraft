Who We Are

A Decade of Excellence in Glass & Aluminum

HexaGlaze is a trusted name in the glass and aluminum fabrication industry. With over 10 years of experience, we have been delivering precision-crafted solutions to homeowners, architects, builders, and businesses across the region.

Our state-of-the-art fabrication facility is equipped with modern machinery and staffed by skilled professionals who are passionate about quality. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver products that exceed expectations.

Our message: every project we take on is a promise — to measure honestly, fabricate precisely, and install with care. We would rather build a relationship that lasts for years than complete a one-off transaction.

Before You Buy a Frameless Door

Aug 25, 2026 7 min read Doors
Door Planning Guide

Get the measurements and hardware right

A frameless glass door relies on its hinges, pivots, and clamps to carry the panel securely. Hardware must be rated for the glass weight and suited to the wall or floor where it will be fixed.

Check the opening for plumb walls, a level floor, and enough clearance for the door to swing without striking nearby fixtures. These details affect both the final panel size and how smoothly the door operates.

Have the opening measured on site before fabrication, and confirm the glass specification and hardware finish together. Careful planning helps avoid fit issues once the panel is delivered.
